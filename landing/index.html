Questions, answered.

The things every EBMS shop asks before switching on [Product].

Does [Product] replace EBMS?

No. [Product] sits on top of your EBMS. EBMS stays the system of record — every order, customer, and document change syncs straight back.

How long does onboarding take?

About an hour. Connect your EBMS credentials, pick the modules you need, invite the team. No migration, no downtime.

Is my data safe?

Yes. Data is encrypted in transit, access is role-based, and we only talk to your EBMS through its API — nothing touches your database directly.

Can I customize invoices and packing lists?

Yes — that's the visual document builder. Drag fields onto the page, bind them to live data, and print from any record. Invoices, packing lists, shipping labels.

We run multiple businesses — does that work?

Multi-project is built in. Switch businesses from the top bar; superadmins get a cross-project overview of everything at once.

What does the storefront integration do?

It pushes your products and inventory to online storefronts — and you watch every sync land in real time. Successes, retries, errors: one feed.
